**Title**: Quantifying Anisotropic Lagrangian Dispersion and Scaling Regimes in Large-Scale Solenoidal Turbulence

**Description**: This study quantifies the anisotropy of passive tracer dispersion in subsonic ($M \approx 0.07$) isothermal turbulence by comparing Mean-Square Displacements (MSD) parallel and perpendicular to the dominant solenoidal driving modes ($n=1-3$). By integrating 5,000 Lagrangian tracers across 200 evenly spaced snapshots using RK4 sub-stepping, we isolate the influence of large-scale forcing on the diffusion tensor, specifically measuring the ratio $\lambda = MSD_{\parallel} / MSD_{\perp}$ to identify preferential transport pathways. We will test the hypothesis that the solenoidal driving geometry induces a transient ballistic-to-superdiffusive crossover, characterized by a time-dependent power-law exponent $\alpha(t)$ that deviates from Brownian motion ($\alpha \approx 1$) due to persistent large-scale velocity correlations. To ensure statistical rigor within the $\approx 4$ eddy turnover time constraint, we compute the displacement distribution tails to detect non-Gaussian signatures of coherent structure interactions. This approach replaces the ill-posed integral scale estimation with a robust characterization of the flow's inherent anisotropy, providing a concrete metric for how solenoidal injection at $n=1-3$ dictates the spatial evolution of tracers in a periodic subsonic box.
