- Decision: ITERATE
- Justification: The current project is at a high-value inflection point. The results are already scientifically sound and provide a compelling contrast to 2D turbulence, but the manuscript currently suffers from minor interpretative inaccuracies (e.g., mislabeling geometric saturation as subdiffusion) and a static anisotropy metric that weakens the argument. These are not fundamental flaws in the data or the simulation, but rather "review-readiness" issues. 

The proposed changes—specifically correcting the scaling analysis to account for periodic saturation, implementing a dynamic anisotropy metric, and linking FTLE ridges to Lagrangian Coherent Structures (LCS)—will significantly increase the "Acceptance Delta." These improvements transform the paper from a descriptive study into a rigorous dynamical systems analysis of 3D turbulence. The ROI is high because the data is already generated and the code infrastructure is in place; the effort required is primarily analytical and editorial rather than computational. By executing these changes, the paper will move from a "Borderline" to a "Strong Accept" by satisfying the rigorous standards of top-tier fluid dynamics journals (e.g., JFM or Physics of Fluids), where the distinction between geometric artifacts and physical transport regimes is critical.
