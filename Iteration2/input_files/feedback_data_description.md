The observed failures in the analysis—specifically the late-time saturation of the Mean-Square Displacement (MSD), the artifactual drop in the power-law exponent $\alpha(t)$, and the emergence of platykurtic displacement distributions—are directly attributable to the constraints described in the dataset description.

1. **Constraint**: The simulation domain is a small, periodic unit box ([-0.5, 0.5]³).
2. **Limitation**: The research plan attempted to track tracers over a time span of ~10 units, which is approximately 4 eddy turnover times. Given the domain size ($L=1$) and the rms velocity ($v_{rms} \approx 0.38$), the tracers reach the geometric saturation limit ($L^2/6$) well before the end of the simulation.
3. **Affected Conclusions**: The analysis of the late-time diffusive regime and the search for anomalous diffusion (Lévy flights) are compromised. The "platykurtic" displacement distributions and the sub-unity $\alpha(t)$ values are mathematical artifacts of the periodic boundary conditions and finite domain size, rather than physical properties of the turbulence. The high boundary-crossing rate (82.9%) confirms that the domain size is insufficient to capture the long-time asymptotic behavior required to test for Lévy flights or confirm the diffusive limit without significant geometric interference.
